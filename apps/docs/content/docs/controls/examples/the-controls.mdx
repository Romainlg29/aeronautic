<Example name="the-controls" />

The fighter stands on its wheels, gear down, at idle. One
`<ControlSurfaces>` drives all of it from the flight the sliders write:

- **The stick** moves the elevons, together for pitch and against each other
  for roll, the stick in the cockpit, and the nozzles' gimbals.
- **The pedals** move the rudders, the pedals in the cockpit and the
  nozzles' gimbals. With the air brake out, they open one side's drag
  rudders further.
- **The throttle** opens and closes the nozzles' petals, moves the throttle
  lever up to its reheat detent and lights the plumes.
- **The flaps** droop the leading-edge flaps: this fighter has no trailing
  edge flaps of its own.
- **The air brake** opens the brakes on the wings and splits both drag
  rudders.
- **The gear** scrubs the model's own retraction clip, its doors and legs in
  the order they were animated, and moves the gear lever.
- **The canopy** isn't in a flight, so it is moved by hand:
  `rig.setClip("ANIM_Canopy_Open", share)`.

Each part moves at its actuator's rate, so the surfaces lag the stick a
little and the gear takes a couple of seconds.

```tsx
const flight = new Flight({ gear: 1, throttle: 0 });

<FlightProvider flight={flight}>
  <primitive object={scene} />
  <ControlSurfaces
    ref={rig}
    object={scene}
    animations={animations}
    exhaust={<Afterburner preset="afterburner" direction={[0, 0, -1]} />}
  />
</FlightProvider>;
```

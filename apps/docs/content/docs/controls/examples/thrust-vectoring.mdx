title: Thrust vectoring

<Example name="thrust-vectoring" />

The fighter's model has a gimbal in each nozzle, `CTRL_Nozzle_L_Pitch` and
`CTRL_Nozzle_L_Yaw` and their right-hand twins, so each `<Engine>` vectors
without being asked. Their extras say `limit_shape: "ellipse"`: pitch and
yaw share one round limit, so full stick and full pedal together stop on the
circle, not at its corner.

- **The stick, pitch** turns the exhausts up as the stick comes back, which
  pushes the tail down and the nose up.
- **The pedals** turn them right with the right pedal.
- **The throttle** opens the petals at idle, closes them down at military
  power and opens them wide in reheat.

The plume is put on each engine's exhaust anchor, so it turns with the
nozzle. The readout comes from `on_vector`, called in the frame the nozzle
turns:

```tsx
<ControlSurfaces
  object={scene}
  animations={animations}
  engine={{ on_vector: (pitch_deg, yaw_deg) => show(pitch_deg, yaw_deg) }}
  exhaust={<Afterburner preset="afterburner" direction={[0, 0, -1]} />}
/>
```

See [Engines](../../guides/engines/) for capping the limits, turning
vectoring off, or giving a model with no gimbal one.

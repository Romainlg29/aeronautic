<Example name="atmosphere-plume" />

One engine under [`@takram/three-atmosphere`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@takram/three-atmosphere)'s
sky. Drop the sun to the horizon and the camera opens up for the dimmer
sky, so the same flame comes up brighter against it. Climb, and the sky
darkens over thinner air while the plume reads the same flight's air.

The setup, the volume pass with the aerial perspective as its backdrop and
the scene's place on the earth, is on [core's page](/docs/core/integrations/takram-atmosphere).
For the plume, one number: its exposure.

## The plume's exposure

The atmosphere draws in units of its `luminanceScale`, one unit on screen
being about 75 700 cd/m². A plume's radiance is in units of a 2000 K
blackbody's, about 4.8 × 10⁵ cd/m², so in the atmosphere's units it is drawn
at

```ts
import { AtmosphereParameters } from "@takram/three-atmosphere/webgpu";

const exposure = 4.8e5 * new AtmosphereParameters().luminanceScale; // ≈ 6.3

<AfterburnerBatch preset="afterburner" profile={{ exposure }}>
  <Afterburner />
</AfterburnerBatch>
```

whatever the preset was graded at. The camera's own exposure, metered from
the sky, does the rest.

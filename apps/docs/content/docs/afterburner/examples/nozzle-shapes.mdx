<Example name="nozzle-shapes" />

The same engine through four exits. The oval and the flat slot are params
(`nozzleAspect`, `nozzleSquareness`). The lobed exit is the `outline` hook,
in a batch of its own because a hook belongs to its batch. Roll them, and
stretch how far downstream the shape lasts before the jet mixes round. See
[Shaped nozzles](../../guides/nozzle-shapes/).

Each can is extruded from the same outline its plume measures against, so the
flame fills the rim it leaves.

<Example name="rocket-mix" />

A `propellant` param instead of raw soot numbers. Rich (low O/F) is dark, smoky and sheathed in fire; lean is clean, with the diamonds showing.

`Stage` and `Nozzle` are the [shared stage](../basic-jet/#the-stage).

<Example name="countermeasures-altitude" />

The night example, twelve kilometres up, and the altitude yours to change.
Everything that changes with it comes from the standard atmosphere's
pressure, temperature and density there:

| altitude | burns for | falls at, slowed | at the grain as thrown at 250 m/s | chaff sinks at |
| -------- | --------- | ---------------- | --------------------------------- | -------------- |
| 0 m      | 2.9 s     | 28 m/s           | 92 kcd                            | 0.18 m/s       |
| 6 km     | 3.2 s     | 39 m/s           | 129 kcd                           | 0.22 m/s       |
| 12 km    | 3.4 s     | 56 m/s           | 172 kcd                           | 0.28 m/s       |
| 15 km    | 3.6 s     | 71 m/s           | 194 kcd                           | 0.31 m/s       |

- **It burns longer** in thinner air: MTV's burn rate goes with the pressure
  to the 0.094.
- **It falls faster** once slowed, its drag going with the air's density,
  and is slowed less: higher up a program's flares stream further back.
- **The airstream strips less** off the flame at the same airspeed, so it
  dims less as it leaves: an estimate, as on the night page.
- **Chaff sinks faster**, Lamb's drag on each fibre falling with the air's
  density, and its fibres lie a little further from level.
